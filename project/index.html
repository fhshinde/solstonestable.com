Why we do this

We spend our working lives bringing established businesses up to date. The same work — a clean site that loads quickly, reads properly on a phone, and tells people plainly what you do — costs a small charity money it would far rather spend on the cause itself.

So for non-profits we do it for nothing. Not a stripped-back version, and not a template with your logo dropped on top. The same care we'd give a paying client.

What you get

A site built for you.

Designed around what your organisation actually needs people to do — donate, volunteer, find you, understand the work.

Works on a phone.

Most people will meet you on a small screen. It will load quickly and read properly there, not just on a desktop.

Yours to keep.

Your domain, your hosting, your content. No lock-in, no monthly fee to us, and nothing held hostage if you'd rather move on.

Simple enough to run.

We'll show someone on your team how to update it, so you're not waiting on anyone to change an opening time or add an event.

Who it's for

Registered non-profits, charities and community groups doing real work with limited means. If your website is holding you back — or you've never had one — we'd like to hear from you.

We take on a small number at a time so each one gets proper attention, so there may be a wait. We'll always tell you honestly where you stand rather than leave you wondering.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about your cause.

    A short email is plenty. What you do, who you serve, and what's not working.

  2. Step 2

    We talk it through.

    Thirty minutes to understand what the site actually needs to achieve for you.

  3. Step 3

    We build it and hand it over.

    Then we show your team how to run it, and it's yours.

Want to chip in?

We cover the cost of these builds ourselves, and we'd rather keep it that way — no organisation we help will ever be asked for money.

But the number we can take on is limited by what we can absorb. If you'd like to help us say yes to more of them, you're very welcome to contribute. Every dollar goes into the next build — hosting, domains, and the time to do it properly.
